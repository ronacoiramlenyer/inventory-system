Quick Guide

For Lab Custodians

Everything you need for your everyday work in the laboratory, explained simply.

1. Logging In

  1. Open the Lab Management System website.
  2. Type in the username and password given to you by your admin.
  3. You'll land on your Dashboard — a summary of your laboratory.

2. Opening Your Laboratory

  1. Click Laboratories on the left menu.
  2. Click your laboratory's name from the list.
  3. You'll see a row of tabs across the top — these are all the forms for that laboratory (Inventory, Equipment, Schedules, Requests, and more). Click any tab to open that form.

3. Counting Your Inventory

This is the Inventory Sheet — where you record how much of each supply you actually have.

  1. Open your laboratory, then click the Inventory Sheet tab.
  2. For each item, type in the quantity you actually counted.
  3. Click Save if you just want to keep your count as a draft for now.
  4. Click Apply Adjustments to Stock when you're sure the count is final — this updates the official stock balance so it matches what you counted.
New item you don't see on the list? You can add it directly on this page, then count it the same way.

4. Recording Equipment

This is the Equipment Monitoring Record — the logbook for machines and tools in your lab.

To add a new piece of equipment:

  1. Open your laboratory, then click the Equipment Record tab. You'll see a list of your lab's equipment.
  2. Click + Add Equipment and fill in its name, serial number, and location.
  3. Click Save.

To log a service entry for equipment that's already listed:

  1. Click that equipment's name in the list to open its own record.
  2. Click + Add Entry and fill in the date, what service was performed (Preventive, Repair, or Calibration), the related Work Request ID (if any), and the status.
  3. Click Save Entry.

5. Maintenance & Calibration Schedules

  1. Open your laboratory, then click the Schedules tab (Preventive Maintenance or Equipment Calibration).
  2. Click + Add Row.
  3. Choose the equipment from the dropdown — its details fill in automatically.
  4. Fill in the scheduled date, then Save. Once the work is actually done, come back and fill in the actual date.

6. Requesting an Equipment Repair

  1. Open your laboratory, then click the Work Requests tab.
  2. Click + New Request and describe the problem.
  3. Submit it. Your Subject Coordinator will review and approve it, then it goes to the office to get worked on.
  4. You can check back on this same page to see its status (Pending, Filed, In Progress, Completed).

7. Borrowing Something

The left-menu Borrowing Requests page only lists requests from every lab — to file a new one, start from your own laboratory.

  1. Open your laboratory, then click the Borrowing Requests tab.
  2. Click + New Request and fill in the borrower's name, department, date needed, return date, and purpose.
  3. Click + Add Item for each item being borrowed, then submit.
  4. Your Subject Coordinator approves it directly, and it's ready to print.
  5. Print it, have the borrower sign it by hand, then scroll down to Signed Hardcopy and click + Attach Signed Copy to upload a photo of the signed printout — that's the official record.

8. Logging Waste Disposal

  1. Open your laboratory, then click the Waste Log tab.
  2. Click + Add Entry and describe what was disposed of, how, and by whom.
  3. Click Save Entry. No approval needed — it's recorded right away.

9. Reporting an Incident

  1. Open your laboratory, then click the Incident Report tab.
  2. Click + New Report and describe what happened.
  3. Submit it. It gets its own reference number automatically, and shows who filed it once signed.
  4. Print it, have those involved sign it by hand, then scroll down to Signed Hardcopy and click + Attach Signed Copy to upload a photo of the signed printout — that's the official record.

10. Printing a Form

  1. Open the form you want to print.
  2. Click the Print button near the top of the page.
  3. Your browser's print window will open — check that it looks correct, then print.
Tip: If you see an extra line of text (like a web address or date) at the top or bottom of the printed page that doesn't belong there, open "More settings" in the print window and turn off "Headers and footers". That extra line comes from the browser, not from the form.

11. If You Get Stuck

  • Can't find something in a dropdown? It probably needs to be added to your laboratory's inventory first.
  • A request isn't moving? It's likely waiting for your Subject Coordinator or the office to act on it — that's normal, just check back later.
  • Still stuck? Ask your Subject Coordinator or your system admin for help.

Technical Reference

For Admins & IT

Full detail on roles, workflows, form codes, and system behavior. Not needed for everyday use.

How Printing Works

Clicking Print hides the sidebar and on-screen controls and opens the browser's print dialog for that form only.

  • The F-LAB header (school seal + "page X of Y" label) repeats on every physical page a form spans.
  • The form code and revision line (e.g. F-LAB-006 Rev. 0) prints in the footer of every page.
  • Log-style forms (Stock Cards, Equipment Record, Schedules, Waste Log, Work Requests) pad in blank rows even when there are only a few real entries, so the printout fills a page the same way the paper original does.
  • The "page X of Y" label is an estimate based on row count, since browsers don't expose an exact page-count API — it's close but not guaranteed exact for entries with unusually long notes that wrap onto extra lines.

Request Approval Chain

Equipment Work Requests F-LAB-004 / F-LAB-005 and Other Requests (Bookstore / Supplies / BGU) share the same chain:

  1. Staff files a request — status starts as Pending.
  2. The lab's Subject Coordinator approves it, moving it to Filed.
  3. It appears in the cross-department inbox for any Secretary to pick up.
  4. Secretary sets it to In Progress, then Completed or Rejected.

Borrowing Requests F-LAB-007 are simpler: the Subject Coordinator approves directly, stamping a "Digitally signed by" line tied to their real account — no separate filing/secretary step.

Admin: Edit Item / Correct Balance

On the Stock Card page, admins get two extra actions next to Delete Item:

  • Edit Item — change name, category, unit of measure, reorder level, or notes.
  • Correct Balance — type the actual counted quantity from a physical/manual count; the system posts the signed difference as a normal transaction (same mechanism the Inventory Sheet's Apply uses), so the correction stays visible and auditable in the ledger rather than silently overwriting history.
Note: Equipment is intentionally excluded from Stock Cards, since that form is specifically the consumable quantity ledger. Equipment has its own record — F-LAB-001.

Admin Tools

  • Departments — manage the department list laboratories belong to.
  • Staff Accounts — create/manage user accounts and roles.
  • Laboratory approval — approve newly enrolled laboratories from the Laboratories page.

Troubleshooting / FAQ

My printout has the browser's own header/footer overlapping the form's.

Turn off "Headers and footers" in your browser's print dialog (under "More settings").

The equipment I need isn't in the schedule dropdown.

Add it first from that laboratory's Inventory (Equipment category) — the Maintenance/Calibration dropdowns pull directly from there.

I don't see the Equipment Work Requests inbox.

It only shows for Secretary and Admin accounts, since it's the cross-department fulfillment queue.

A request I filed is stuck on "Filed."

It's waiting for a Secretary to pick it up and move it to In Progress — reach out if it's been sitting a while.
