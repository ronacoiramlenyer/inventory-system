Overview

Lab Management System digitizes La Salle Green Hills' laboratory paperwork — inventory, equipment, schedules, requests, and incident logs — while keeping every printed form byte-identical to the official F-LAB templates.

Roles

RoleCan do
StaffManage their assigned laboratory's forms: inventory, equipment, schedules, logs, requests.
Subject CoordinatorEverything Staff can, plus approve/file requests originating from their laboratories.
SecretaryWorks filed requests (Equipment Work Requests, Bookstore/Supplies/BGU) across every department, moving them to In Progress / Completed / Rejected.
AdminEverything above, plus Departments, Staff Accounts, laboratory approval, and item corrections on the Stock Card page.

Getting Started

  1. Go to the Lab Management System login page and sign in with the username/password your admin gave you.
  2. You'll land on the Dashboard, scoped to your department (or all departments, if you're an admin).
  3. Use the left sidebar to reach Laboratories, Borrowing Requests, Other Requests, and (for secretaries/admins) Equipment Work Requests.
  4. Pick a laboratory to open its own set of forms — Inventory Sheet, Stock Cards, Equipment Record, Schedules, Work Requests, Waste Log.

Opening a laboratory shows a row of form tabs (Inventory Sheet, Equipment Record, Schedules, Work Requests, Stock Cards, Waste Log, Borrowing, Incidents) — this is the fastest way to jump between a lab's own paperwork.

Dashboard

Landing page after login. Shows pending-approval counts (admins), your laboratories, and shortcuts into the forms you use most.

Laboratories

New laboratories are enrolled by staff and then approved by an admin before they become active. Once approved, opening a lab redirects straight to its Inventory Sheet — the natural first stop for any lab.

Inventory Sheet F-LAB-010

The working count sheet for a laboratory's consumable inventory. Two save modes:

  • Save — stores your counted quantities as a draft, without touching the official stock balances yet.
  • Apply — posts the difference between counted and recorded balances as an auditable IN/OUT transaction against each item's Stock Card.

You can also paste a spreadsheet export to bulk-import new items directly into the sheet.

Stock Cards F-LAB-006

The running quantity ledger — one card per item, showing every IN/OUT transaction and the resulting balance. Items are grouped into their own table per category (Supplies, Materials, Chemicals, Glassware, Consumables, Other).

Note: Equipment is intentionally excluded from Stock Cards, since this form is specifically the consumable quantity ledger. Equipment has its own record — see Equipment Record below.

Admin: Edit Item / Correct Balance

On the Stock Card page, admins get two extra actions next to Delete Item:

  • Edit Item — change name, category, unit of measure, reorder level, or notes.
  • Correct Balance — type the actual counted quantity from a physical/manual count; the system posts the signed difference as a normal transaction (same mechanism the Inventory Sheet's Apply uses), so the correction stays visible and auditable in the ledger rather than silently overwriting history.

Equipment Monitoring Record F-LAB-001

Equipment now lives as its own category inside the general inventory (Category = Equipment), and this page is its dedicated monitoring log — usage entries, condition, and remarks per equipment item. Equipment entered here is also what fills the equipment dropdown on the Maintenance and Calibration schedules.

Preventive Maintenance / Calibration Schedule F-LAB-002 / F-LAB-003

Both schedules share the same shape. The Equipment Name & Description field is a dropdown sourced straight from that laboratory's Inventory (Equipment category) — pick an item and its serial number auto-fills. Each row also has a Department field.

Equipment Work Request F-LAB-004 / F-LAB-005

F-LAB-005 is the lab's own log of work requests; F-LAB-004 is a single request's printable detail. Approval chain:

  1. Staff files a request — status starts as Pending.
  2. The lab's Subject Coordinator approves it, moving it to Filed.
  3. It appears in the cross-department Equipment Work Requests inbox for any Secretary to pick up.
  4. Secretary sets it to In Progress, then Completed or Rejected.

Borrowing Request F-LAB-007

Single-step approval: the lab's Subject Coordinator approves directly, which stamps the form with a "Digitally signed by" line tied to that coordinator's real account — no separate filing/secretary step.

Waste Disposal Log F-LAB-008

Straightforward log, no approval step — add an entry per turnover with classification, quantity, and disposal method.

Incident Report F-LAB-009

Each report gets an auto-generated reference number and, once filed, a "Digitally signed by" stamp on the printout.

Other Requests (Bookstore / Supplies / BGU)

Same Pending → Filed → Secretary chain as Equipment Work Requests, grouped under one top-level "Other Requests" nav item with a lab picker.

Admin Tools

  • Departments — manage the department list laboratories belong to.
  • Staff Accounts — create/manage user accounts and roles.
  • Laboratory approval — approve newly enrolled laboratories from the Laboratories page.

Printing & the Print Button

Every printable form page has a Print button near the top of the page, next to the "+ Add…" button. Clicking it opens your browser's normal print dialog for that form only — the sidebar and every other on-screen control are automatically hidden from the printout.

What the Print button gives you

  • The official F-LAB header (school seal + "page X of Y" label) repeats automatically on every physical page a form spans, not just the first.
  • The form code and revision line (e.g. F-LAB-006 Rev. 0) prints in the footer of every page.
  • Log-style forms (Stock Cards, Equipment Record, Schedules, Waste Log, Work Requests) pad in blank rows even when there are only a few real entries, so the printout fills a page the same way the paper original does.

Troubleshooting / FAQ

My printout has the browser's own header/footer overlapping the form's.

Turn off "Headers and footers" in your browser's print dialog (under "More settings") — see the Printing section above.

The equipment I need isn't in the schedule dropdown.

Add it first from that laboratory's Inventory (Equipment category) — the Maintenance/Calibration dropdowns pull directly from there.

I don't see the Equipment Work Requests inbox.

It only shows for Secretary and Admin accounts, since it's the cross-department fulfillment queue.

A request I filed is stuck on "Filed."

It's waiting for a Secretary to pick it up and move it to In Progress — reach out if it's been sitting a while.
